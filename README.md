HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes employee attrition and workforce trends for 1,470 employees.

## Objective
To understand why employees leave and which groups are most affected, so HR can make better retention decisions.

## Key Metrics
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Overall Employees | 1,470 |
| Attrition (employees who left) | 237 |
| Attrition Rate | 16.12% |
| Active Employees | 1,233 |
| Average Age | 37 |

## Dashboard Features
- KPI cards for headcount, attrition, attrition rate, active employees and average age
- Department-wise attrition (pie chart)
- Number of employees by age group and gender (stacked column chart)
- Job satisfaction rating by job role (matrix)
- Education field-wise attrition (bar chart)
- Attrition rate by gender across age groups (donut charts)
- Education level slicers to filter the whole report

## Key Insights
- R&D has the highest attrition (133 of 237 employees, 56.12%), followed by Sales (92, 38.82%) and HR (12, 5.06%).
- Most employees are in the 25-34 and 35-44 age groups.
- Life Sciences and Medical education fields have the highest attrition.
- Research Scientists and Laboratory Technicians are the largest job roles.
